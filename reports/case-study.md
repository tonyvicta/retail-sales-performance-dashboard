## Business problem
Leadership needs a single view of sales performance to spot which categories and stores are over- or under-performing week to week. In a Retail & Consumer Services context, this is the kind of question a Customer Insights Analyst is expected to answer with data rather than intuition.

## Approach
I worked through this in clear stages:
1. Period-over-period sales change by category and store
2. Top and bottom performers with likely drivers
Tools used: Excel / Spreadsheets, Power BI. All data was public or synthetic. No confidential employer data was used.
